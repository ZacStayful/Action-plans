The honest risk in the overlap period

I raised this on the call and I want it written down rather than buried. While guests and tenants share the kitchen and communal areas you have two exposures: your tenants living alongside people they did not choose, and guests forming their first impression of a shared space you do not control on the day.

What genuinely reduces it in your case: every room is ensuite, so guests have a private bathroom and never queue for one. You told me the kitchen is immaculate and your tenants are working professionals. And your tenants turn over roughly every six months anyway, so the overlap is a phase, not a permanent state. Tell your tenants before the first guest arrives, not after. A stranger with a suitcase is a problem; an expected guest is not.

Your questions, answered honestly

What happens to our money if Stayful goes under? That is our biggest worry.+
It is the right question to ask and it deserves a straight answer. Booking revenue is held in a separate holding account, not in Stayful's own trading account. It is your money being held on your behalf — the same principle a lettings agent operates under. Stayful's revenue is only the management fee it deducts. So if the company hit trouble, the money owed to you is not a general company asset to be swallowed up.

What I will not tell you is that this eliminates counterparty risk entirely, because no structure does. What you can do: check MWB Stays Ltd at Companies House, read clause 21 of the management agreement on payout timing, and ask us for owner references before you sign. If we could not stand up to that, we would not be asking you to sign.
How do we get out, and what happens to bookings already taken?+
Six-month initial fixed term, then a rolling three-month notice period, either side. When you give notice we agree a cut-off date. No new bookings are taken beyond it, existing guests are honoured and paid out normally, and the property hands back cleanly on that date.

One thing I should have flagged on the call: if you terminate inside the initial six months, the agreement carries a £1,000 early exit fee (clause 1). I would rather you hear that from me now than find it in the small print. The fixed term exists because a brand-new listing needs time to build reviews — but you are entitled to know the number.

There is no guarantee attached to any of these numbers, and I am not going to pretend otherwise.
Will we lose the HMO licence — and can we ever get it back?+
You told me the council has said the seven-bed licence will not be re-granted once surrendered. Take that at face value: this is a one-way door, and it is the single most important thing on this page.

That licence is real value in the property, both as income optionality and at resale. My honest advice is not to surrender it a day earlier than you have to. A phased room-by-room transition keeps it live while you find out whether short-letting actually suits you. Please take your own planning advice before any formal change of use — I am a management company, not your planning adviser.
Will maintenance be marked up on top of the invoice?+
No percentage mark-up on contractor invoices. We authorise urgent repairs up to £300 in a month without troubling you; above that we come to you for approval first, with a quote where there is time. Guest-caused damage comes out of the stay deposit or, if it is serious, our insurance. Fair wear and tear is the owner's cost, as it would be under any arrangement.
Council tax or business rates — and does the phased approach change it?+
It might, and this is worth more detail than I gave on the call. To move from council tax to business rates in England the property must be available to let commercially for at least 140 days in the current and previous year, and actually let for at least 70 days in the previous 12 months.

That matters for you specifically: while you are letting one or two rooms alongside sitting HMO tenants, you may not clear those thresholds, so plan on the council tax position continuing in the interim. Once you are at whole-property short-let and clearing the thresholds, the switch becomes available — and at your likely rateable value small business rate relief should mean nothing to pay. Get it confirmed with the VOA rather than relying on me.
