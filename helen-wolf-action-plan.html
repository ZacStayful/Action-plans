Questions answered honestly

How often do you actually inspect the property — and would this have caught the garden issue you mentioned?▾
Yes, structured inspections happen 2–4 times a year, and any issue flagged by a guest message or cleaner gets logged and actioned the same day rather than waiting for the next scheduled visit. A garden looking untended for "quite a while" is exactly the kind of thing that shows up in the changeover-clean checklist before it ever reaches a guest complaint.
You're based in the Midlands — how will you manage a Bath property better than a local agent?▾
Honestly, no management company is genuinely "local" in the sense of the account manager living round the corner — and your own experience shows that even a Bath-based agency can let standards slip once it's absorbed into a larger group. What actually matters is the inspection cadence and how fast issues get actioned. Stayful already coordinates cleaner and maintenance networks in Bristol and Margate on the same model that would apply in Bath.
How many properties do you manage in Bath specifically?▾
We didn't have the exact figure to hand on the call — Zac will confirm this by email. To be transparent, Stayful's Bath footprint is smaller than a specialist purely-local agency's might be. What we bring instead is a proven nationwide system (cleaning, maintenance, review strategy) across ~70 properties, rather than a single-city operation.
What happens to my existing reviews and booking history if I switch?▾
This is genuinely worth checking before any decision — if your current agent's listing is under their own Airbnb account rather than yours, reviews and history may not transfer automatically. Worth raising directly with them on your visit next week. If the listing is under your own account, it should carry across when we take over management.
What is the honest worst case if these projections aren't hit?▾
The worst case is occupancy comes in below the 56% area average — most likely if the listing effectively restarts without its existing reviews. That's exactly why the Year 1 figures are already discounted by 20% against Year 2. Even in a soft month, a well-presented property in this location should still clear the £1,747/month long-let benchmark comfortably, unless something more fundamental is wrong — which is closer to the risk your current situation already carries with a neglected garden and an unresolved guest complaint.
What if I decide to stay with my current agent after my visit?▾
That's completely fine — no pressure at all. You mentioned they seem to have picked up a little recently, and it's entirely reasonable to give them a fair chance to prove that. Stayful is happy to just be an option you can come back to any time if things don't improve.
