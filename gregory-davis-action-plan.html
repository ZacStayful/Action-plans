Your questions, answered honestly

How does the listing migration actually work — will I lose my reviews?⌄
No — your 80 reviews and 4.84★ rating transfer with the listing. You agree a booking cut-off date with your current company, we deactivate briefly during your quiet October/November window, then our Airbnb account manager relists using your existing history rather than starting fresh. You keep your logins and can update the account email to complete the handover.
Why is my current revenue so much lower than these projections?⌄
Mainly two things: the review gap from October 2024 to around September 2025 with almost no new reviews, and pricing that's roughly 20% above comparable Lincoln listings. Both are fixable — dynamic pricing corrects the second one immediately, and consistent bookings plus active review management rebuild the first.
What happens during the deactivation window?⌄
You'll have a short period with no live bookings while the listing transfers. That's exactly why we've picked October or November — already your quietest months — so the real income impact is minimised rather than costing you peak-season nights.
What about the front door lock confusing guests?⌄
You've already solved this — the backup lockbox with a fob is the right fix, and we'll keep using it rather than relying solely on the automated pin lock.
What's the honest worst case if projections aren't hit?⌄
Bookings and nightly rates can run below projection, particularly while reviews rebuild toward the 128+ target. Our Year 1 figure already reflects that conservatively. The worst realistic case is Year 1 net landing below £27,546 for a period — not below your long-let benchmark of £10,292, which the numbers above it are built to comfortably clear even in a soft month.
What if I keep my London property with a different manager?⌄
Entirely your call — this agreement covers Museum Court only. If you'd like Stayful to look at the London property too, just say so and we'll run the same analysis on it separately.
