Your questions, answered honestly
What is the honest worst case if the projections aren't hit? ▾

Year 1 is the nervy year — we need to build reviews and establish your ranking. You should expect 15–20% below our projected average for the first 5–7 months. That still puts you above what you were getting on long-let, but it's not instant. The real risk is if the market shifts significantly or if there's an issue with the property that affects reviews early. If something does go wrong we'll tell you plainly rather than spin it.

How does the 3rd bedroom (box room) affect the figures? ▾

The figures shown are for a 3-bed listing. If the box room can't accommodate a single bed, it lists as 2-bed, which changes the competitor set and would reduce these projections. The impact isn't catastrophic but it's meaningful — likely £3,000–5,000 less annually. Zac will send a 2-bed comparison alongside these figures so you can see both scenarios.

What happens if I want to stay at the property myself? ▾

You have full access to block dates in your owner calendar at any time. Confirmed guest bookings always take priority — you can't cancel a booking that's already confirmed — but any open dates can be blocked in advance. We'll set up your access from day one so you can manage this whenever you're back in the UK.

Who handles damage, insurance, and maintenance costs? ▾

Guest-caused damage: if a guest breaks something, we first pursue recovery through platform channels or the guest deposit. If not recovered, the platform's AirCover (up to £2.5M) covers major incidents. Fair wear and tear — things that just get old with use — is your responsibility and comes out of monthly statements. We authorise repairs up to £300 without prior approval to keep things moving.

What does the 15% management fee actually cover? ▾

Everything operational: multi-platform listing (Airbnb, Booking.com, Google, direct), dynamic pricing, guest screening and ID verification, check-in/check-out coordination, cleaning and linen coordination, maintenance liaison, monthly statements, Slack comms, quarterly review calls. You handle utilities, council tax, smart thermostat, and key safe — that's your complete list of responsibilities.

What if I want to stop — what's the exit? ▾

After the initial 6-month term, either party can exit with 3 months' written notice. During the first 6 months there's an early exit fee of £1,000. If you need to exit because of genuine circumstances — visa changes, family situation — we'd talk through it rather than hold you rigidly to contract terms.
