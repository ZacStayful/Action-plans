What drives demand in Peckham / SE15

🚇 Transport links

Canada Water and central London are within easy reach, and the area is well served by rail — convenient for groups heading into town.

🏙 Central London on your doorstep

Comparable listings sit within ~3 km around Tower Bridge, Borough Market and London Bridge, so you tap the same visitor demand from a quieter base.

Only six comparable listings within 2.86 km. A 5-bed sleeping 10–12 is rare, and groups and families struggle to find space together.

🎨 Peckham itself

🎓 Events & universities

Events around the Shard and central London plus university visits (e.g. Greenwich) add midweek and shoulder-season demand.

A correction from our call. I mentioned a "short-stay letting licence" from the council. That was wrong: England has no such licence. In Greater London, letting a property short-term for more than 90 nights a year (direct bookings count too) needs planning permission for change of use, applied for through Southwark Council's planning team. This is a planning application, so timing and outcome are not guaranteed. Please confirm the process and cost with Southwark planning (or a planning consultant) before committing.
