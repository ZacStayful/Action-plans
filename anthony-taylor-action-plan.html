Frequently asked

Why keep 4 beds instead of converting to a 5th bedroom? +
A games room is harder for competitors to replicate — it needs 2 reception rooms, 2 bathrooms and 4+ beds, which most similar listings don't have. It also commands a higher nightly rate and attracts higher-quality guests than squeezing in a 5th bedroom would.
How reliable is the 12-month ramp-up estimate? +
It's a genuine range, not a guarantee — reviews and search ranking on Airbnb typically take about 12 months to fully establish, and income in months 1-3 will sit meaningfully below the full run-rate figure. We've built that ramp into the Year 1 numbers above rather than hiding it.
Should I rely on platforms or direct bookings? +
Both, working together. We build the platform presence first since that's what earns reviews and ranking — direct bookings are layered on top as an optimisation once the listing is established, not a replacement for it. Relying solely on either channel carries more risk than a dual approach.
What happens with guest damage? +
AirCover provides up to £2.5M in damage protection plus theft cover, and Stayful manages every claim directly with Airbnb — you're never left dealing with it yourself.
What is the honest worst case if projections are not hit? +
Worst case in our monthly range is around £1,900 net in a slow month, and if the ramp-up runs at the low end you could see closer to £1,500-1,800/month in the first quarter while reviews build. Occupancy and rate can also be affected by wider market conditions that are outside anyone's control. We'd rather show you that range honestly now than have it be a surprise later.
