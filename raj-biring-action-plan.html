Your questions, answered honestly

Are you sure about these numbers?+
As sure as comparable market data allows — we typically see this land within about 10% accuracy, built from 12 active listings of similar size within 0.34km of your property. It's not a guarantee, but it's not a guess either.
If guests are mostly contractors on weekdays, will weekends sit empty?+
No. The comparable data already reflects normal weekend leisure demand alongside any weekday business stays — which is exactly why the figures hold up across the whole week, not just Monday to Friday.
Will this definitely cover my mortgage and running costs?+
Based on the comparable data, no month is modelled as loss-making against your £680/month mortgage — even the quietest month (February, Year 1) nets roughly £1,822.
How is the property protected without interior cameras?+
A £400 security deposit on every booking, ID checks and cross-platform history review on every guest, and insurance cover for anything serious. Check-in and cleaning are both tracked, giving more oversight than a standard tenancy.
What's the honest worst case if projections aren't hit?+
Being direct: the first year could run 10–20% below even the conservative Year 1 figures shown here while you build your first reviews from zero against an established market average of 173 reviews at 4.9★. Nothing in the comparable data points to a loss-making month against your mortgage — but if performance runs meaningfully below plan once you're live, we'd revisit pricing and positioning with you rather than just wait it out.
