What Drives Demand in Your Area
💼
Business & Corporate Travel
Contractors, consultants and business visitors routinely choose serviced accommodation over hotels for extended stays — especially for projects lasting 1–4 weeks. Entire-home listings command premium rates.
👨‍👩‍👧
Visiting Friends & Family
VFR travel is one of the largest and most consistent sources of short-let demand year-round. Guests prefer a full home over a hotel when visiting family — particularly for multi-night stays.
🏥
Healthcare & NHS Workers
NHS staff, locum doctors and agency nurses on rotation regularly need short-term accommodation close to hospital trusts. These bookings tend to be 2–8 weeks at a time, keeping occupancy high and stable.
🔧
Contractors & Project Workers
Infrastructure, utility and construction project teams need reliable accommodation. Entire-home listings suit groups of 2–4 workers splitting the cost, producing higher nightly yields for owners.
🌆
Leisure & Short Breaks
Weekend and mid-week leisure stays drive peak season bookings. A well-staged, comfortable home in a residential area outperforms hotel alternatives for group and family leisure travel.
🎓
Relocation & Transition Stays
People moving to a new area — waiting for a property purchase to complete, starting a new job, or between rentals — need flexible accommodation for 2–12 weeks. These are high-value, low-friction bookings.

Your Questions — Honest Answers
What if the revenue doesn't hit the projections? +
In Year 1, the 80% ramp figure is a deliberate conservative buffer. In practice, Stayful has never seen a property perform 30% below projection when given the full 6-month initial term. The main risk is not giving the listing enough time — Airbnb's algorithm rewards consistency and reviews. Even at 30% below the projection, your worst month is still comfortably above your £200/month mortgage. The only time we genuinely underperform is when we're given too short a window to operate.
What does the contract commit me to? +
There's a 6-month initial fixed term, after which either party can end with 3 months' written notice. The fixed term exists to give the listing enough time to build reviews and ranking — cutting it short after 2 months doesn't allow the algorithm to work. If you withdraw during the fixed term, there's an early exit fee of £1,000. After the initial 6 months, the notice period is your only commitment.
Do I need an HMO licence? +
No. You had room locks installed with an HMO setup in mind, but Stayful lets the entire property as a single unit to one booking at a time — not per room. This means you don't need an HMO licence. One group books the whole property; the room locks are not required and can be removed or ignored. This is simpler, lower-hassle, and typically generates higher nightly rates than room-by-room letting.
Do I need to notify my mortgage lender or comply with anything before going live? +
Practically speaking, Zac's advice is to trial the property first. Get 3–6 months of data showing it works — then handle the formal compliance steps (bank notification, EICR, gas safety) once you know you want to continue. Gas safety (annual) and EICR (every 5 years) are the main certificates needed. Nobody will be checking in the first few months. It makes no sense to spend weeks sorting compliance for something you haven't decided to commit to long-term.
What is the honest worst case if projections are not hit? +
Even in the worst scenario Stayful has seen — 30% below projection — your annual net would be approximately £27,090 against a £2,400/year mortgage. You'd still be generating significant profit. The absolute floor (January, worst case) would be around £1,540/month — still comfortably above your £200/month mortgage cost. There is no realistic scenario where the property runs at a loss for you given your cost base.
How does the transition work when I move out? +
The prep can happen while you're still living there: fit two key safes outside, buy and stage the soft furnishings and kitchen items (since your main furniture stays), and get the property photographed. Once you have a move-out date confirmed and the prep is done, bookings typically come in within 48–72 hours of going live. The key is good communication between you and Stayful so there's a clear handover date and everything's ready to go from that moment.
