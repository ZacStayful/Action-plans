What drives demand in Torquay
🏖
The English Riviera Brand
Torquay is the heart of the English Riviera — one of the UK's most recognised domestic holiday destinations, drawing millions of visitors annually seeking quality seaside breaks without travelling abroad.
☀
UK's Warmest Coastal Climate
Torquay records some of the highest sunshine hours and mildest coastal temperatures in England, meaningfully extending the shoulder season and supporting bookings well into October.
⛵
Royal Regatta & Maritime Events
Torbay hosts the prestigious Royal Regatta and numerous sailing events throughout the summer — fixed-date events creating predictable demand spikes bookable months in advance.
📚
Agatha Christie Heritage
Torquay is the birthplace of Agatha Christie, attracting literary tourists year-round. The heritage trail, Torquay Museum, and Greenway house pull a distinct visitor demographic even in low season.
🚶
South West Coast Path
The 630-mile SWCP runs through Torbay, making Torquay a natural walking and cycling base — a secondary year-round demand stream beyond pure beach tourism.
🍴
Dining, Harbour & Nightlife
Torquay's working harbour and surrounding streets have a well-established restaurant and leisure scene, making it attractive to couples and groups seeking a UK city-break alternative.

Your questions — honestly answered
Can I still try to sell the property while you manage it?+
Yes — completely. You keep full access to the booking calendar and can block dates for viewings. Confirmed guest bookings take priority, but the only coordination needed is checking the calendar before scheduling viewings. Many owners run the property on the market and on Airbnb simultaneously. The income makes the wait more comfortable.
What about the council tax situation?+
Once operating as a short-let, you can apply to reclassify the property from residential to commercial/business rates. With a single property you should qualify for Small Business Rates Relief — typically bringing your liability to zero. It takes 6–12 months to process, but the refund is backdated from the reclassification date. That alone saves you over £7,200 per year at your current £601/month rate.
What is the honest worst case if projections are not hit?+
Torquay is highly seasonal — your peak months of June, July, and August represent the bulk of annual income. In genuinely difficult years, the realistic floor is roughly: winter months £1,200–£1,500 net, peak months £4,000–£4,500 net. Your property has strong fundamentals (two bathrooms, parking, dual balconies, sun all day) that protect against the bottom end. The honest worst case is still substantially better than the current situation of the property sitting empty at £10,000+ per year in costs.
How does guest damage actually work?+
Every guest pays a £200 security deposit before receiving check-in instructions — minor damage or missing items come from this. For anything larger, guests are liable under Airbnb's AirCover programme (up to £2.5m). We coordinate all repairs through our contractor network. In practice, serious damage is rare, but when it does happen the process works. We manage the entire claim — you are never on the phone to Airbnb.
What happens at the end of the 6-month term?+
After 6 months the agreement continues on a rolling 3-month notice period. Either party can end it with 3 months written notice — whether because you have sold the property or for any other reason. No exit fees after the initial term.
Is the 13% rate introductory or permanent?+
Permanent. If you sign before 31 July 2026, the 13% + VAT rate is your fee for as long as you are with us. The standard rate is 15% + VAT. Once locked in it does not change unless we both agree otherwise.
