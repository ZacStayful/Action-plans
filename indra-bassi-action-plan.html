The council tax point from our call

You raised this directly, and it's the single biggest lever on your bills. Held in your personal name the flat is treated as a second home, which in Bristol means the premium rate. If it's available to let at least 140 days a year and actually let for 70, it can be reclassified from council tax to business rates — and with a low enough rateable value it may qualify for Small Business Rates Relief, which for many one and two-bed flats brings the bill to nil. Zac suggested a limited company as part of that route.

This is a genuine saving worth chasing — but it's a tax and structuring decision. Please take it to your accountant. We manage property; we're not tax advisers, and we won't pretend the relief is automatic.
