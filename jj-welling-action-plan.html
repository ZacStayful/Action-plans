Demand

What drives bookings in Welling

🚆
Direct trains into central London
Welling station is on Southeastern's Bexleyheath line, with direct trains to London Bridge and Charing Cross in roughly half an hour. Guests get London at a fraction of central-London prices — and with space central London can't offer.
🟣
Elizabeth line at Abbey Wood
Abbey Wood station is a short drive away and puts Canary Wharf, Liverpool Street, the West End and Heathrow on one line — plus ExCeL London via Custom House, which brings exhibition and conference visitors.
🏛️
Greenwich on the doorstep
Greenwich — the Royal Observatory, Cutty Sark and Old Royal Naval College — is a short drive west, a major draw for families visiting London.
🏥
Hospitals nearby
Queen Elizabeth Hospital in Woolwich and Queen Mary's Hospital in Sidcup are both close by — families visiting patients and healthcare staff on placements need somewhere to stay.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
Large homes with parking are scarce
A 5-bedroom house with a garden and off-street parking suits extended families and groups visiting London together. The 12 active comparables within ~6.5 km average around 60% occupancy.
🧳
90+ night stays
Relocations and project teams working across south-east London and Canary Wharf book longer stays — the kind of 90+ night booking that sits outside the 90-night short-let cap.

Protecting the house
🛡️

Damage protection — honestly, by channel

Every booking: £350 security deposit taken before check-in details are released · ID check on every guest · cleaner photographs the house after every clean · no-party policy in our terms · full inspection 3–4 times a year or when complaints flag an issue.

Airbnb bookings: AirCover for Hosts adds host damage protection (up to US$3m, around £2.5m) and covers theft of your belongings by guests. Stayful manages the claim for you — you never deal with Airbnb yourself.

Booking.com & direct bookings: there's no AirCover equivalent. Booking.com's Partner Liability Insurance covers third-party claims, not damage to your home. Here, protection is the deposit, Booking.com's damage claim process against the guest, and your own short-let insurance. I said otherwise on the call — you were right.

Booking.com provides Partner Liability Insurance, which covers claims from third parties (for example a guest injury, or damage to a neighbour's property), but it has no AirCover-style damage protection for your own house and contents. On Booking.com and direct bookings, protection comes from the £350 security deposit, ID checks, our cleaners' after-clean photo record, Booking.com's damage claim process against the guest, and your own buildings & contents insurance — which needs to be a policy that covers short-let use. The deposit is designed to cover minor damage and act as a deterrent — it's collected before check-in details are released, so a guest can't arrive without it. Being honest: on non-Airbnb bookings, larger damage relies on your insurance, so it's worth checking your policy before go-live. A higher deposit is possible, but it does reduce bookings from perfectly good guests — happy to talk through what level you'd be comfortable with on the next call.
