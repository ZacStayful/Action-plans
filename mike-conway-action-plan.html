What drives demand in Cheltenham

🏇

Cheltenham Racecourse

Festival week in March is one of the biggest events in the British sporting calendar and fills the town. The November Meeting and New Year's Day racing add more. Groups travel together and want one big house.

🎭

The town's festivals

Cheltenham runs Jazz (spring), Science (June), Music (summer) and Literature (October) festivals every year, bringing visitors across the calendar, not just race weeks.

🌳

Gateway to the Cotswolds

Charlton Kings sits at the foot of the Cotswold escarpment, with Leckhampton Hill and the Cotswold Way close by. Families and friends book large homes for walking weekends and get-togethers — the summer peak in the figures.

🏥

Cheltenham General Hospital

Under a kilometre away. Visiting families, agency and locum staff and medical contractors need somewhere nearby — a steady, year-round source of longer stays, especially for the flat.

🎓

Schools & university

Cheltenham College, Cheltenham Ladies' College and Dean Close bring parents in for open days, exeats and fixtures, and the University of Gloucestershire campus is about 2 km away.

🏰

Very few homes this size

Most short-lets nearby take far fewer guests. A house sleeping 12 is one of a handful that can take a large group — and those are the bookings that command the highest nightly rates.
