Control over the asset

You block the calendar yourself when you want the property. You see every booking, every rate and every deduction as it happens rather than a rent figure once a month.

Service you'd actually stand behind

You built and sold a lettings business — you know what "doing the basics" looks like. This is a different operating model, and you'll be able to judge it against your own standard within weeks, not years.

Upside once established
