What drives demand in Glastonbury

⛰️

The Tor, the Abbey & Chalice Well

Some of the best-known heritage sites in the South West, all walkable from the town centre. They draw visitors in every month, not just summer.
🔮

Holistic & spiritual visitors

Glastonbury's Isle of Avalon reputation pulls guests from across the world — your own guests this week came from Spain and the US. This is steady, year-round demand, and often longer stays.
🎟️

A year-round events calendar

As you pointed out, events run through winter and summer — from gatherings and conferences to seasonal fairs like the Frost Fayre. 27 venues and event spaces sit within 5 km.
🎪

Glastonbury Festival

At Worthy Farm in Pilton, a few miles away. It's one week in late June with periodic fallow years — a big rate spike when it runs, but a bonus, not the base.
🛍️

Street, Wells & the Levels

Clarks Village in neighbouring Street, the cathedral city of Wells a short drive away, and the Avalon Marshes — famous for winter starling murmurations — all widen the reasons to stay.
A correction from the call. I suggested Glastonbury demand is mostly the festival week. You were right to correct me — the town has events and visitors all year round. That's reflected here, and it's why the quietest month still nets around £1,600.
