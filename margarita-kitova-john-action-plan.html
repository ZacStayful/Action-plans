The counter-intuitive part is that a higher interest rate produces a lower monthly cost, because you stop repaying capital every month. That is the whole mechanism — and it is what turns £2,361 a month of net letting income into something that actually clears.

What the restructure costs

Stamp duty on transfer

~£42,000

At a valuation around £500,000. This is the big number and there is no way around it — the sale into the SPV is a purchase for stamp duty purposes.

Funded from

Existing equity

The equity already in the property can potentially cover both the stamp duty and the deposit, so the new cash you need to find may be far smaller than £42,000.

Also budget for

Legal + broker

Conveyancing on the transfer, broker fees, and accountancy time to set the SPV up correctly.

Please do not take this as tax advice. Moving a property into a company has capital gains and income tax consequences that depend entirely on your circumstances. Your accountant needs to sign this off before you commit to anything. What we can do is present the letting income to a lender in the format they want to see.

On the Granlea application

You mentioned a previous mortgage attempt on another property that did not get through. From what you described it sounds like a presentation problem rather than a fundamental one — the figures were not packaged in the way a lender needs to read them. When you come to apply through the SPV, we will put the income projections together in lender-friendly format so the underwriter is looking at the same picture we are.

What waiting costs

The two tracks are separable. You can list the property and restructure the finance afterwards — and there is a good argument for doing exactly that, because it gives the lender real income history instead of a forecast. But every month you are not live is a month of income you do not get back.

3. Cranleigh, how we work, and the proof

Why the bookings come, what we actually do, and real numbers from our own portfolio.

🎓

Cranleigh School & Cranleigh Prep

Around 960 pupils across the senior and prep schools, with roughly 575 of them boarding. Boarding schools generate a steady, repeating accommodation need — drop-off and pick-up weekends, exeats, open days, speech day, sports fixtures and parents visiting from overseas. It is one mile from the village centre and it is demand that recurs to a school calendar rather than the weather.

⛰️

Surrey Hills National Landscape

Cranleigh sits on the southern edge of a designated Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. Walking, cycling and riding visitors treat the village as a base, and this is the demand that fills the spring and autumn shoulder months rather than just high summer.

🚴

The Downs Link trail

The 37-mile Downs Link runs along a former railway line from St Martha's Hill outside Guildford down to Shoreham-by-Sea, and Cranleigh has direct access to it. Cyclists and long-distance walkers doing it over two days need somewhere to stop, and a whole property with somewhere to store bikes is exactly what they look for.

🏙️

Guildford and the commercial corridor

Guildford is eight miles away, roughly twenty minutes by car, with Horsham a similar distance the other way. That puts contractors, project teams and business visitors within easy reach at a price point well below Guildford town centre — and those bookings tend to be midweek, which is where most short lets are weakest.

🎪

Village events calendar

The annual Cranleigh Show, the Cranleigh Bonfire with its torchlight procession down the High Street, the Thursday farmers' market and a regularly programmed Arts Centre all pull people into the village on specific dates. The bonfire in particular falls right around your target go-live.

🚗

No station — so parking matters

Cranleigh has no railway station; the line closed decades ago and the village is served by buses from Guildford, Horsham and Godalming. In practice almost every guest will arrive by car. That is not a negative — it means off-street parking is a genuine ranking and rate advantage — but it does shape who books and how the listing should be written.

Your questions, answered honestly

Not in a property like this, no. Room-by-room short letting only works where each guest has their own genuinely separate living space and bathroom. Without that you get strangers sharing a kitchen and a lounge, which produces exactly the kind of guest conflict that generates one-star reviews — and reviews are the single thing that determines how fast your listing climbs. The whole-property figures in this plan assume single-party bookings, and that is what we would list.

You are currently on a residential mortgage on a repayment basis, so every monthly payment includes capital as well as interest. That is what is eating the profitability, not the letting income. The route we discussed is to set up an SPV limited company, sell the property into it, and take a buy-to-let mortgage through the SPV on an interest-only basis. The interest rate will almost certainly be higher — assume north of 6%, possibly closer to 8% — but because you are no longer repaying capital each month, the monthly outgoing comes down materially. SPVs are entirely standard now and most buy-to-let lenders will lend to a newly incorporated one.

The honest answer is that it is not cheap and you should go in with your eyes open. On a valuation around £500,000 the stamp duty on transferring the property into the company is in the region of £42,000. There will also be legal fees and broker fees on top. The offsetting point is that the equity already in the property can often cover both the stamp duty and the deposit, so the amount of new cash you need to find can be much smaller than the headline number. Your accountant needs to sign off the tax position before you commit — this is a decision with capital gains and income tax consequences, and I am not qualified to advise on those.
