What drives demand where you are

You are on the North Bristol fringe, about three miles outside the city boundary. That position is the asset here — these are the specific reasons a guest books Rudgeway over central Bristol.
🚗
The M4/M5 interchange on your doorstep

Almondsbury is roughly ten minutes away, where the M4 meets the M5. That single junction puts London, the Midlands, the South West and South Wales all within easy reach and makes the house a natural overnight stop for people who are driving through rather than into Bristol.

🏢
North Bristol business corridor

Aztec West and the Bristol Parkway corridor sit between you and the city — the main cluster of business parks north of Bristol. Your own enquiry flagged business and contractor guests, and that is exactly the demand this location serves: midweek, multi-night, low-maintenance stays.

🏙
Bristol without Bristol prices

Fifteen minutes to the city centre means guests get everything they came for and still park on a private drive. For groups and families that trade is usually a better one than a city-centre flat.

✈
Airport and the Severn crossings

Bristol Airport is about 45 minutes, and the Severn crossings put Wales, Chepstow and the Wye Valley within half an hour. Pre-flight and post-flight nights are steady, unglamorous, reliable bookings.

🏚
A genuine touring base

Bath, Cheltenham, the Cotswolds and the Forest of Dean are all comfortable day trips. Guests who want to see the region rather than one city book longer stays, which lifts occupancy in the shoulder months more than peak.

🔒
Very little competing supply

Only twelve active comparables within 13.63 km, and the average listing there carries 36 reviews at 5.0 stars. Four bedrooms, private parking and genuine privacy with no overlooking is a combination almost nothing else in that radius offers.

On your point about reliability

You said you were not sure the information you gave was precise enough to calculate to a degree of reliability. That is a fair challenge. The model runs on bedrooms, bathrooms, parking and address-based competition — which is why it can be run before I have seen the house. What it cannot yet price is finish quality, and yours is well above the local average. Once your photos and plans arrive, the numbers get revised, and on a near-Passive-House new build with a double garage and no overlooking, the revision is more likely to go up than down. I would rather show you the unrevised figure now than a flattering one.

Why October matters

Not a deadline, and there is no offer attached to it. It is the review clock, and it is the one genuinely time-sensitive thing in this plan.

It takes five to seven months for a new listing to reach the local average of 36 reviews. Live in early October and you are competitive by around March and at full strength for the July peak — the month worth £5,460 net. Push the go-live to spring and you arrive at next summer with no review history, which is the single most expensive way to start.
