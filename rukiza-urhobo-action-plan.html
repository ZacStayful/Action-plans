What drives demand around Powell Street

💼
Business & professional travel
Birmingham is the UK's largest city economy outside London. The Colmore business district and Brindleyplace host major banks and professional-services firms, including HSBC UK's headquarters — a steady source of weekday stays for visiting staff and consultants.
🏗️
Contractors & regeneration
The HS2 Curzon Street station works and large regeneration schemes across the city bring contractor teams who need multi-week, self-catering stays. A 2-bed with a kitchen suits crews sharing — the guest type you flagged yourself.
🎤
Events & conventions
The ICC, Symphony Hall and Utilita Arena are close to the city centre and run conferences, concerts and shows through the year. Big exhibitions at the NEC also push overflow demand into city-centre accommodation.
🚆
Rail connections
Birmingham Snow Hill is roughly 1.2 km from the flat, with New Street — one of the UK's busiest interchange stations — a short hop further. Easy rail access matters a lot for business and weekend visitors arriving without a car.
🏥
Hospitals & universities
Birmingham Children's Hospital is in the city centre, and Aston University and Birmingham City University are nearby — bringing families visiting patients, visiting academics, open days and graduation weekends.
🎄
Winter & weekend leisure
The Jewellery Quarter's bars and restaurants give the area strong weekend appeal, and Birmingham's Frankfurt Christmas Market draws large visitor numbers through November and December — consistent with the strong winter months in your figures.
