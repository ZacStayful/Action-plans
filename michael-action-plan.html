Your questions, answered honestly

How do I know these figures aren't just to get me enticed?▾
Fair question — you asked it directly on the call. These numbers come from real listings within a 10km radius of your address, weighted for a 4-bedroom property, not a generic city average. Zac's also personally reviewing the Buckley Brown photos of your actual property to confirm whether £2,500/month average holds, is conservative, or could go higher.
What is the honest worst case if projections are not hit?▾
The local market has genuinely low competition (only 52 reviews on average) but that also means it can take 6–12 months to build up reviews and reach the average figures shown here — that's exactly why Year 1 is shown at 80% of the full run rate. If the market underperforms further, your net could sit closer to the low end of the range (around £1,985/month before the 80% reduction) rather than the average. It should still clear your guaranteed-rent offer of £900/month, but it isn't impossible for a quiet month to come close. There's a 6-month fixed term, then a 3-month rolling notice, so you're not locked in indefinitely if it isn't working.
You mentioned properties 150 miles away — how does key/access security work if I can't manage it myself?▾
You won't need to. We run two separate key safes — one for guests (rotating code, changes between stays) and one for cleaners/maintenance — so no single code is shared with everyone. We handle all code changes remotely; you never need to visit.
Do I need a licence for short-term letting?▾
Not currently in Nottingham (London has additional rules, but that doesn't apply here). Regulation is likely to tighten over time — fire safety and registration schemes are the most probable next steps — but there's no licence requirement today.
Can the management fee come down further, given my long-let rate is 7%?▾
Short-let has more moving parts than long-let (dynamic pricing, guest screening, cleaning coordination, 24/7 comms) which is why the baseline is higher. Zac's already offered 13%+VAT (down from the standard 15%) for signing before 7 August, and is checking internally on a further 12.5%/12% structure as you build up towards 3+ properties — he'll confirm that separately.
