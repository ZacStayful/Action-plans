Your questions, answered honestly

Q: Will the reported plans for nearby temporary housing put off leisure visitors?
A: We don't think so. Most people visiting Bicester Village or the wider area aren't going to be deterred by this, and if anything, local short-term accommodation needs often increase around this kind of development. We'll keep an eye on it as your listing beds in.
Q: What happens with maintenance costs — I've been overcharged by contractors before?
A: We completely understand that concern, especially on a one-bed where costs bite harder. We work to a £300/month pre-approval threshold — anything above that, or anything unusual, gets flagged to you on Slack before we spend it. Genuine emergencies (like avoiding a bad review) may need paying at emergency rates, but routine issues can often be bundled with other jobs in the area to bring costs down. We budget on maintenance typically running under 5% of gross, though on a one-bed it's sensible to treat that 5% as a real buffer rather than a rare cost.
Q: Do you offer partial management, or is it full management only?
A: Full management only, at 15% + VAT of gross. We've found that without full control of the property day-to-day, it's very hard for us to consistently deliver the standard of guest experience that protects your reviews and income.
Q: What's the minimum stay, and what should I expect on average?
A: Two nights minimum — one-night stays generally aren't profitable once cleaning costs are factored in. We aim for an average stay of 7–12 nights, which reduces turnover costs and cleaning frequency. One-bed properties can sometimes see slightly more turnover than larger ones, but longer stays are still very achievable, including from the hospital-related demand mentioned above.
Q: My white goods insurance can take 2–7 days to respond — is that workable?
A: Worth flagging honestly: that response time can be too slow for guest expectations, especially with a live booking in progress. We'll note your existing cover on your onboarding form and, where sensible, get a cheaper assessor out first to keep things moving, saving your insurer for the actual repair.
Q: What's the honest worst case if these projections aren't hit?
A: Being direct — year one is a ramp-up year, and we're genuinely upfront that it usually takes around 4 months to reach full run rate as your listing builds reviews and visibility (something that's actually in your favour here, since local review counts are currently low). Figures can land up to 10% either side of what's projected. The realistic worst case is a slower few months than hoped while the listing establishes itself, not a collapse in income — and we'll flag early if performance is consistently tracking below plan rather than let it run quietly.
