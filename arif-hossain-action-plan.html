What drives demand around Bentley

🏇
Doncaster Racecourse

One of Britain's oldest racecourses and home of the St Leger in September, plus fixtures through the year. Race days fill accommodation across the town, and groups prefer a whole house over hotel rooms.

🚆
East Coast Main Line

Doncaster station sits on the main London–Edinburgh line, roughly 1 hour 40 minutes to King's Cross, with fast links to Leeds, York and Sheffield. It makes Doncaster a practical base for business travellers and visitors.

🦺
Contractors & logistics

Doncaster is a major distribution hub, with large warehousing and the iPort rail-freight site south of town. Contract and project workers need multi-night stays with a kitchen and parking — exactly what a 2-bed house offers.

🏥
Doncaster Royal Infirmary

The main acute hospital for the borough brings visiting families, agency staff and locums who need somewhere comfortable for days or weeks at a time.

🦁
Yorkshire Wildlife Park

A big family day-out attraction near Branton, a short drive away. It drives weekend and school-holiday demand from families who want space and a sofa bed rather than a cramped hotel room.

📉
Light local competition

The average competing listing has around 33 reviews. Under 100 is a sign of an unsaturated market, which is why we're confident a well-run, well-reviewed listing can reach these numbers.

A correction to what I said on the call: a company let on its own doesn't make the property qualify. In England a property is assessed for business rates as self-catering when it's available to let for at least 140 days a year and actually let for at least 70 days in the previous year. Once it qualifies, Small Business Rate Relief can often bring the bill to zero for a property of this size. So the route is: run it as a short-let for a year, show those thresholds were met, then apply. Running it through a limited company is a separate tax decision worth checking with an accountant — it isn't what triggers the switch.
