Frequently Asked Questions

What is the honest worst case if projections are not hit? +
Occupancy or nightly rate can come in below projection in a soft market, particularly in the early months before reviews build. The floor case is closer to the long-let net figure (around £10,012/year) rather than a loss — Stayful does not guarantee bookings, and income projections are estimates, not contractual commitments.
How quickly can the property go live? +
Typically 2-4 weeks from signing, covering any setup work, professional photography, and listing build across Airbnb, Booking.com and Google Hotels.
What happens if a guest damages something? +
AirCover provides up to £2.5M in damage protection and Stayful manages the entire claim on your behalf — you are never expected to deal with Airbnb directly.
Can I still use the property myself? +
Yes — personal use is simply blocked out in the calendar in advance. We can talk through how many weeks make sense once you know your own plans.
What is included in the management fee? +
Guest communication, pricing, cleaning coordination, maintenance liaison, monthly reporting and photography are all covered — see the 'What Stayful Handles' section above for the full list.
