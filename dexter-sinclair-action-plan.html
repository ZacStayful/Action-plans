Your questions, answered honestly

Q: How do you manage the property if I'm in Vietnam?+
A: Everything runs through us end-to-end — guest communication, check-in/out via your digital lock, cleaning, and maintenance coordination — with a dedicated Slack channel for anything you want visibility on. You don't need to be reachable at odd hours; we handle the day-to-day and only flag you for genuine decisions.
Q: What kind of guests will actually book — will it suit events and weekend visitors?+
A: Yes — Saltaire's mix of transport links, nearby event venues, and heritage appeal draws exactly that profile. We screen all guests before booking and can apply house rules suited to a residential street.
Q: My cleaner already knows the property — can we keep using them?+
A: Yes, we're happy to vet your existing cleaner against our framework — we'll just need a per-changeover price that includes linen, supplies, and before/after photos, so quality is consistent whoever's cleaning.
Q: What's the honest worst case if these projections aren't hit?+
A: Saltaire is a genuinely competitive, well-reviewed market, so the realistic downside is a longer ramp-up — more like 12–15 months to full occupancy rather than 12, and a Year 1 net closer to the low end of our range if reviews build slower than expected. Even in that scenario, based on the local comparable data, short-let still outperforms the £10,012/yr long-let benchmark.
Q: What exactly is my ongoing involvement once we're live?+
A: Day-to-day, effectively none — we run bookings, guest comms, cleaning and maintenance. You'll get a monthly statement and can check the live calendar any time. We'll only come to you for approvals above your agreed repair threshold or genuine judgment calls.
