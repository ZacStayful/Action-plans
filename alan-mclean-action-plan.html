What drives demand around Buckingham Road

🚆

Direct trains to Manchester

You described a 5–10 minute walk to Stalybridge station. Direct TransPennine trains reach Manchester Piccadilly in around 15 minutes, with onward services to Leeds, Liverpool and Manchester Airport. Guests can stay here and be in the city quickly.

🏞️

Edge of the Peak District

Stalybridge borders the Peak District National Park. Stalybridge Country Park, the Pennine Bridleway and Dovestone Reservoir are all close, which suits walkers, cyclists and families.

🏟️

Manchester events

The Etihad Stadium, Co-op Live arena and the National Cycling Centre sit on the Manchester side of the line. Match, concert and event days push up demand for places to stay within easy reach.

📊

A market that's already proven

Your analysis found 12 active Airbnb listings within about 11 km, averaging 56% occupancy and about £67,700 a year. They also average 110 reviews, so a new listing needs a strong start, which is why Year 1 is shown at 80%.

🏠

House plus annex

A 3-bed house with a self-contained 1-bed annex gives you flexibility most listings don't have: a big group or family together, or guests who want their own space. You mentioned the annex could suit corporate stays, and we'll test that against real demand.

Parking and privacy
