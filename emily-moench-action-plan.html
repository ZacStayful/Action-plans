What drives demand around Bodiam

🏰
Bodiam Castle

The National Trust's 14th-century moated castle is on the doorstep — one of the best-known heritage sites in the south-east, drawing day-trippers who want to stay the weekend and explore the area.

🚂
Kent & East Sussex Railway

The heritage steam line runs between Tenterden and Bodiam. Family days out and seasonal special services bring visitors throughout the year, not just in summer.

🌳
High Weald countryside

The cottage sits in the High Weald National Landscape — protected countryside with walking, cycling and country pubs. This is the classic "weekend escape" market for couples, families and friends' groups.

🏖️
Rye, Battle & the coast

Medieval Rye, Battle Abbey (site of the 1066 battle), Hastings and Camber Sands are all within a short drive, so guests use the cottage as a base for touring East Sussex and Kent.

🍇
Vineyards, gardens & weddings

Sussex wine country, Great Dixter's gardens at Northiam and the area's rural wedding venues bring weekend groups who need a larger house — exactly the 4-bed, whole-home stay that's scarce locally.

🚆
London within reach

Robertsbridge station, a short drive away, has direct trains to London in roughly an hour and a quarter — easy enough for Friday-to-Sunday stays from the capital.

The management agreement

Your questions, answered honestly

A professional clean is done after every checkout. The team has a 5-hour changeover window between one guest leaving and the next arriving, and fresh linen is included in every clean. Cleaning is charged to the guest as part of the booking, and we schedule every changeover automatically from the booking calendar — you don't need to organise anything.
Our honest recommendation is not to offer it to guests. An open fire or log burner used by people unfamiliar with it is one of the bigger fire and insurance risks in a holiday let. Many rural cottages keep the burner as a feature but take it out of guest use. Whatever you decide, there should be a carbon monoxide alarm in that room, the chimney should be swept, and your holiday-let insurer needs to know about it. We'd rather put the effort into features guests value and can use safely — a well-set-up living room, good heating and a smart thermostat.
Our essential item checklist covers everything a guest-ready property needs — kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, linen and safety items. As you're refurbishing anyway, it's worth working through it alongside the refurb so nothing needs to be bought twice. If you'd rather we handle furnishing, we can quote for a full or partial setup once you know what's staying.
Nothing we discussed stops the cottage operating as a short let, and England has no short-let licence requirement. Before going live you'll need: an up-to-date fire risk assessment, smoke and CO alarms, a fire blanket and first aid kit, gas and electrical safety certificates where applicable, and holiday-let (short-let) insurance. We'll run through these with you during onboarding.
Utilities, WiFi and council tax stay with you, and they aren't deducted in the net figures above — so budget your roughly £1,000 of bills against the net income. A smart thermostat (around £200 one-off) helps keep heating costs in check between stays. Once a holiday let meets the annual availability and letting thresholds, it can be assessed for business rates instead of council tax, and small business rate relief may reduce that — worth checking at the time, but not guaranteed.
Year 1 is genuinely slower — the listing starts with no reviews, which is why we plan on 80% of the forecast (£35,935). If every month performed like January, the cottage would net around £2,440/month — still above the £1,170/month long-let figure, though after your bills the margin would be much thinner. None of this is guaranteed. The agreement has a 6-month initial term (with a £1,000 early exit fee in that period), then runs on a 3-month rolling notice — so if it isn't working, you're not locked in long-term.
The standard management fee is 15% + VAT of rental revenue (after platform fees), plus a £42/month software fee. There's no charge until you're live and earning. The initial term is 6 months, then 3 months' notice on either side. Full details are in the agreement and walkthrough video above.
