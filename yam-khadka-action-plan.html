What drives demand in DN2

🏇
Doncaster Racecourse
Town Moor sits right in DN2, a short distance from the property. Race days through the year — and especially the St Leger Festival in September — fill local accommodation with racegoers and groups who want a whole house.
🏥
Doncaster Royal Infirmary
The town's main hospital is also in DN2. Visiting families, agency staff and medical professionals on placement book mid-length stays all year round, which smooths out the quieter months.
👷
Contractors & logistics
Doncaster is a major distribution and logistics hub, with large warehouse parks and ongoing construction work. Contractor teams want a 3-bed house with parking for weeks at a time — several local comparables market directly to them.
🚆
East Coast Main Line
Doncaster station is on the main London–Edinburgh line, with fast links to London, Leeds, Sheffield and York. That brings business travellers midweek and visitors exploring Yorkshire at weekends.
👨‍👩‍👧
Family visits & events
Larger homes suit family gatherings, weddings and visits to friends and relatives — demand that hotels serve poorly. The Eco-Power Stadium and Doncaster Dome add sports and leisure weekends.

Your questions, answered honestly

Not quite the same, and we need to correct what was said on the call. In England, a short let already has legal safety requirements: a valid gas safety certificate (checked every 12 months by a Gas Safe engineer) and a written fire risk assessment are both required today, along with working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms. An EICR isn't strictly required for short lets in England the way it is for long-let tenancies, but we'd still recommend one — insurers often ask for it. On top of that, the government's national short-let registration scheme is expected to start in March 2027. If you go ahead, we'll keep the property compliant as the rules land.
Yes — upholstered furniture you supply (sofas, sofa beds, mattresses, headboards, cushions) must meet the UK furniture fire safety regulations and carry the fire-safety labels. New furniture from mainstream suppliers, including Sublime Furnishings who we use, is compliant as standard. Beyond that, there's no set "short-let style" — the aim is that a guest arrives with just their luggage and has everything they need, whether it's two nights or three weeks.
This is the right first check. Many standard buy-to-let mortgages are written for an assured shorthold tenancy, so you'll need your lender's written consent to short-let — some agree straight away, some add conditions or a small rate change, and a few say no (in which case a holiday-let mortgage is the usual route). For insurance, a standard landlord policy won't cover short-let guests — you'll need a specialist short-let/holiday-let policy. We can point you to providers our owners use.
