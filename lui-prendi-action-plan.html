What drives demand around BN2 4TF

🌊

Seaside city with year-round pull

Brighton is one of the best-known seaside destinations in England. The seafront and pier draw leisure visitors through the summer and a steady flow of short breaks the rest of the year.

🎪

Festivals and big events

Brighton Festival and Fringe in May, Brighton Pride in the summer and the Brighton Marathon in spring all bring visitors who need places to stay, often in groups.

🚆

Quick rail link to London

Brighton station has frequent direct trains to London, so the city works for weekends away, work trips and day-trip overflow from the capital.

🎓

Two universities

The University of Sussex and the University of Brighton generate repeat visits from parents, graduation guests and visiting academics.

🏥

Major hospital nearby

The Royal Sussex County Hospital is in the Kemp Town area of Brighton, which creates demand for stays from patients’ families and visiting medical staff.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

A four-bed suits groups

Larger homes are scarcer than hotel rooms or flats. Families and friends travelling together book the whole house — that is where your nightly rate comes from.

Your nearby competition

12 active Airbnb listings within about 3 km average a 5.0★ rating and 116 reviews, a £489 average nightly rate, 71% occupancy and £102,246 a year in revenue. The market is proven — your listing would start with no reviews, which is why Year 1 is set at 80%.
