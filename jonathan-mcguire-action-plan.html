One of the UK's biggest independent flower shows, held every August and drawing tens of thousands of visitors to the town.

⛳ Royal Birkdale & links golf

Royal Birkdale, on your doorstep, has hosted The Open, and the Sefton coast links draw golfing groups through the season.

🎡 The seafront & Lord Street

One of the UK's longest piers, Pleasureland and the Lord Street shopping boulevard give leisure guests plenty to do all summer.

Beaches, dunes and the red squirrels at Formby make the area a draw for walkers and families.

🚆 Liverpool within reach

Direct Merseyrail trains run from Southport to Liverpool city centre, so guests can base themselves here and still do the city.
