Frequently asked questions

We're not central Milton Keynes near the train station — will that hurt the numbers? +
Not necessarily. Being central isn't the only driver of short-let demand — your location benefits from strong transport links, proximity to Milton Keynes Hospital, and nearby universities, bringing in corporate and NHS bookings as well as leisure guests. That mix is genuinely one of the reasons we like this property's demand profile.
Are the projected figures based on comparable properties near you? +
Yes — the £55,000 gross / £28,500 net projection is based on five comparable short-let properties within half a kilometre of your home. Competition in your immediate area is low (5 comparable listings found, versus a typical ceiling of around 12), which works in your favour.
Does the kitchen and bathroom condition affect the numbers? +
Not necessarily — the property doesn't need to be immaculate, just well-presented. Once you send over your current listing photos, Zac will confirm whether the bathroom is worth a light refresh before going live. A full kitchen renovation isn't recommended given your shorter timeframe.
Do we need planning permission or to notify the council? +
No formal planning permission or council notification is required. We'd recommend the usual safety basics — a fire extinguisher, fire blanket, and working smoke alarms.
What happens with the 6-month commitment if the house doesn't sell in time? +
Our standard agreement is a 6-month minimum term with a 3-month notice period. Given how slow the market is right now, that's unlikely to be a real constraint — but if you'd rather have full flexibility from day one, a notice-only contract is available for a one-off £400 + VAT, with no fixed term.
What is the honest worst case if projections are not hit? +
Being upfront: short-let income isn't guaranteed and fluctuates month to month — our own modelling shows a low of around £900 net in March against a strong £3,300 in April, July or August. It typically takes 3–8 months for a brand-new listing to build reviews and reach its full projected rate. If bookings underperform for a sustained period, your net could sit meaningfully below the £1,900–£2,375 monthly average we've projected. You're not locked in forever though — the notice-only option keeps your exit flexible if it's genuinely not working out.
