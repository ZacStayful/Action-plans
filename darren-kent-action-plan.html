Council tax and business rates, corrected

On the call we described the switch to business rates as 9–12 months, with a refund of interim council tax and a final bill of zero. After checking the published rules, we cannot promise that. Cornwall Council charges a 100% premium on second homes from 1 April 2025. A property moves to business rates only once it has been available to let for 140 nights and actually let for 70 nights in the previous 12 months, as decided by the Valuation Office Agency. Government guidance does not provide for backdating on new lets, and the bill under business rates depends on rateable value and any reliefs. The full detail is in the FAQ in section 4. We have included about £6,000 a year of council tax in our after-costs numbers until the switch.

On the call we said the switch to business rates takes 9–12 months, that council tax paid in the meantime is refunded, and that your liability ends at zero. Having checked the published rules, that was too neat, and here is the corrected picture.

  • Cornwall Council charges a 100% council tax premium on second homes (furnished properties that are not your main home) from 1 April 2025, which is why you see roughly double.
  • A property is assessed for business rates instead of council tax only if it meets three tests: available to let commercially for at least 140 nights in the next 12 months, available for at least 140 nights in the last 12 months, and actually let for at least 70 nights in the last 12 months. The Valuation Office Agency decides.
  • For a brand-new listing, that means council tax continues until the letting history exists. Government guidance says there is no special backdating for new lets, so a refund of council tax already paid is not something to count on. We will check with Cornwall Council and the VOA before you plan around one.
  • Business rates are not automatically zero. What you pay depends on the rateable value and any reliefs you qualify for (small business rate relief may apply). We have not confirmed that, so treat zero as possible, not promised.
  • We could not verify the 9–12 month timescale. Treat it as unconfirmed.

This is why our figures include about £6,000 a year of council tax until the switch happens. We are not tax advisers, so please check your position with the VOA or an accountant. Sources: Cornwall Council second homes page and the government response on self-catering business rates.
